How it works: Each robot receives phase θᵢ = (i · 1/φ) mod 1. Its charging window opens when the global clock phase matches θᵢ. No queue, no arbiter, no robot-to-robot messages. Separation guaranteed by the Three-Distance Theorem — collisions at the charging station are mathematically impossible under uniform timing. Complexity per cycle: O(n log n) vs O(n²) for the central queue.
